One of the most frequent questions I get is: *"Omar, do I need a landing page or a full website?"* The short answer: it depends on your business goal. The long answer is in this article.

## Real Difference Between Landing Page and Website

### Landing Page (Single Page)
- **One single page** with focused information
- **One clear goal**: capture emails, sell a product, book appointments
- **No complex navigation**: user scrolls or clicks the main CTA
- **Persuasive design**: copy oriented to conversion, not general information

### Full Website
- **Multiple pages**: home, about, services, blog, contact, etc.
- **Menu navigation**: users can explore different sections
- **Complete information**: full company story, portfolio, team
- **Extensive SEO**: more pages = more opportunities to appear on Google

## When to Choose a Landing Page

### Choose a landing page if:

**1. You're launching a specific product or service**
Example: You're a dentist in Bogotá and want to promote teeth whitening. A landing page focused only on that service will convert better than your general website.

**2. You're running paid ads (Google Ads, Facebook Ads)**
When you pay per click, you want users to land on a page specifically designed to convert, not your homepage where they get lost.

**3. You have a single conversion goal**
- Capture leads for an online course
- Sell tickets for an event
- Book appointments for a clinic
- Download an ebook or resource

**4. You're starting out and have a limited budget**
A professional landing page ($300-$600 USD) is more accessible than a full site ($1,000-$2,000 USD).

### Examples of Colombian businesses that need a landing page:

- **María, image consultant**: Landing for her "Capsule Wardrobe" course — captures emails and sells the course
- **Healthy food restaurant**: Landing to promote new executive lunch menu
- **Specialist lawyer**: Landing for specific "express divorces in Bogotá" service
- **Travel agency**: Landing for "San Andrés all-inclusive — February promotion"

## When You Need a Full Website

### You need a full website if:

**1. Your business has multiple distinct services**
Example: You're a marketing agency offering SEO, social media, web design, and paid ads. You need separate pages for each service.

**2. You want to rank on Google with multiple keywords**
A site with 10 pages has 10 times more opportunities to appear in searches than a landing page.

**3. You need institutional credibility**
If you sell high-value B2B services ($1,200+ USD), clients will want to see:
- Who's behind the business (about/team page)
- Success stories (portfolio)
- Technical blog (demonstrates expertise)
- Detailed services page

**4. Your content changes frequently**
If you have a blog, update portfolio regularly, or have news/events, you need a CMS (content management system).

### Examples of Colombian businesses that need a full website:

- **Construction company**: Multiple real estate projects, needs gallery for each one, location, specifications
- **Medical clinic**: Different specialties, multiple doctors, health blog
- **Software company**: SaaS product with multiple features, pricing page, documentation
- **Fashion store**: Product catalog, categories, filters, checkout process

## My Recommendation Based on Your Stage

### If you're starting out ($1,500-$6,000 USD budget)
**Start with a professional landing page.**
- It's faster to launch (1 week vs 1 month)
- Allows you to validate your offer before investing more
- You can add pages later if you grow

### If you already have traction ($6,000-$25,000 USD budget)
**Invest in a full website.**
- Long-term SEO positioning
- Credibility for bigger clients
- Scalability to add services

### If you already have a website and want to grow
**Add specific landing pages.**
- One landing for each main service
- Landings for advertising campaigns
- Landings for different customer segments

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Mistake 1: Building a 20-page website when you only sell 1 service
If you're a freelancer and only do "SEO consulting", you don't need 15 pages. One killer landing page is better than an empty site.

### Mistake 2: Making landing pages for everything without a base website
If someone searches your company on Google and only finds old campaign landings, it looks unprofessional.

### Mistake 3: Thinking "more pages = better"
A 50-page site with mediocre content is worse than a 5-page site with excellent content.
