## 2. Types of online stores and how much each costs

It's not the same selling three digital products as managing a catalog of five hundred references with synchronized inventory, national shipping logistics, and electronic invoicing. That's why I divide projects into three levels:

### Basic e-commerce: from $6,000,000 COP

Ideal for entrepreneurs who are starting and need to validate their idea without a disproportionate investment.

**What does it include?**
- Custom design based on premium template
- Catalog of up to 50 products with variants (size, color)
- Functional shopping cart
- Integration with **Wompi** or **PayPal** for receiving payments
- Optimized product page
- Basic SEO configured
- SSL certificate
- Mobile responsive adaptation
- Hosting and domain for the first year included

This type of e-commerce can be developed with **WordPress + WooCommerce** or with a **Shopify basic plan** plus theme customization. The difference is that with WordPress you are the absolute owner of your store and don't pay transaction commissions to the platform (only to the payment gateway), while with Shopify you pay a monthly subscription in dollars starting from **USD $39/month** plus additional commissions if you don't use Shopify Payments.

**Estimated development time**: 3 to 4 weeks.

### Intermediate e-commerce: from $10,000,000 COP

Designed for businesses that already sell offline and want a robust digital presence, or startups that need more advanced functionalities.

**What does it include?**
- Everything from the basic plan
- Catalog of up to 300 products
- Completely custom UI/UX design (no base template)
- Integration with **Stripe** + **Wompi** (card payments, PSE, Nequi, Bancolombia)
- Admin panel with sales dashboard
- Synchronized inventory management
- Advanced SEO (schema markup, speed optimization, breadcrumbs)
- Integrated blog for content marketing
- Advanced product filters
- Social media integration as sales channel
- Automated transactional emails (purchase confirmation, shipping, etc.)
- Training for store management

**Development time**: 6 to 8 weeks.

### Advanced e-commerce: from $15,000,000 COP

The most complete option for established companies, brands with high sales volume, or projects that require complex integrations with external systems.

**What does it include?**
- Everything above
- Unlimited product catalog
- Integration with **electronic invoicing** (mandatory in Colombia for certain tax regimes)
- Multiple payment gateways working simultaneously
- Integration with ERP, CRM, or logistics system via API
- Multi-language (Spanish and English at minimum)
- WhatsApp Business API integration for customer service and notifications
- Advanced reports and analytics panel
- Real-time shipping calculator with Colombian carriers (Servientrega, Envía, Coordinadora)
- Loyalty program and custom coupons
- Core Web Vitals certified speed optimization (LCP < 2.5s)
- Extended technical support

**Development time**: 10 to 14 weeks.

## 4. Payment gateways in Colombia: Wompi, Stripe, PayPal and more

Receiving payments online in Colombia is no longer a headache like five years ago. Today you have very solid options. Here's the comparison of the main ones:

### Wompi (Bancolombia)

Bancolombia's payment gateway has become the favorite of Colombian entrepreneurs for one reason: **it only charges per transaction, with no fixed monthly fee**.

- **Commission**: 3.49% + $900 COP per successful transaction (+VAT)
- **Payment methods**: Credit and debit card, PSE, Nequi, Bancolombia Button, bank correspondents, cash
- **Payout**: Automatic to Bancolombia account with daily cutoff, money arrives in 1 business day
- **Withholding at source**: Wompi automatically manages withholding on commissions
- **Integration**: REST API, WooCommerce plugin, SDK for custom development
- **Ideal if**: your main bank is Bancolombia and you want the highest possible approval rate for Colombian card payments. Being from the same bank that issues most cards in Colombia, the rejection rate is lower.

The best part? **No monthly cost**. If in January you sold nothing, you paid nothing. That's key for seasonal businesses.

### Stripe

Stripe officially arrived in Colombia in 2025 and is changing the rules of the game. Its API is, without discussion, the cleanest and most modern.

- **Commission**: 3.2% + $1,200 COP per successful transaction (+VAT if applicable)
- **Payment methods**: International and Colombian credit and debit card, PSE (in beta for Colombia), payment links
- **Payout**: Rolling 7 business days to Colombian bank account, automatic transfer
- **Invoicing**: Stripe issues invoice for commissions (useful for deducting costs)
- **Integration**: REST API, Stripe Checkout, official WooCommerce plugin, wide ecosystem
- **Ideal if**: you sell to international customers, need recurring subscriptions, or value an extremely polished payment experience. Stripe's checkout is the fastest on the market.

Stripe doesn't charge a monthly fee, just like Wompi.

### PayPal

PayPal remains relevant for international sales. Many Colombians use it to receive payments from clients in the United States, Europe, and Latin America.

- **Colombia commission**: 5.4% + $1,200 COP fixed per transaction
- **Payment methods**: PayPal balance, credit and debit card (even without PayPal account)
- **Payout**: Withdrawal to Colombian bank account (takes 3 to 5 business days)
- **Exchange rate**: Penalty in currency conversion (approx 2.5% above market rate)
- **Ideal if**: your target customer is international and trusts PayPal more than a local gateway

**Watch out for exchange rate**: if you sell in USD and withdraw in COP, PayPal does the conversion at a rate below the market rate. On large amounts, that difference becomes significant.

### Quick commission comparison

| Gateway | Commission per transaction | Monthly fee | Payout | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Wompi** | 3.49% + $900 COP | $0 | 1 business day | National sales, high approval |
| **Stripe** | 3.2% + $1,200 COP | $0 | 7 days | International, subscriptions, UX |
| **PayPal** | 5.4% + $1,200 COP | $0 | 3-5 days | International, brand trust |
| **ePayco** | 2.99% + $900 COP | $0 | 5-8 days | Low cost, multiple methods |
| **PayU** | 3.49% + $900 COP | Variable | 5-10 days | High volume, rate negotiation |
| **Mercado Pago** | 3.49% + $900 COP | $0 | 2 days | Mercado Libre ecosystem, installments |

My recommendation for 2026: **Wompi + Stripe operating in parallel**. Wompi for the maximum approval rate on Colombian cards, and Stripe for the smoothest payment experience and international sales. Integrating both in your e-commerce costs more in initial development, but you recover the investment with the improvement in conversion rate.

A well-made Colombian e-commerce should offer **at least three payment methods**: PSE (the most used in Colombia after cash on delivery), credit/debit cards, and Nequi. If you only have one method, you're losing between 20% and 35% of potential sales according to CCCE data.

## 5. Hosting and domain costs

If you choose **Shopify**, hosting is included in the monthly subscription. You don't need to contract anything additional. The domain you can buy directly in Shopify for about USD $15 per year or connect it from an external registrar like GoDaddy, Namecheap, or Mi.com.co (the latter bills in COP and issues Colombian invoice).

If you choose **WordPress + WooCommerce** or **custom development**, here are the real costs in Colombia for 2026:

### .com.co Domain

- **Mi.com.co**: $45,000 COP/year (renewal) + VAT
- **GoDaddy**: $60,000 COP/year (first year usually has discount)
- **Namecheap**: ~$12 USD/year (approx $49,200 COP at current exchange rate)

A .com.co is the best option if your market is 100% Colombian, both for price and for local trust perception and geolocalized SEO positioning.

### Hosting for e-commerce

Not just any hosting works for e-commerce. You need hosting **optimized for WooCommerce** or, if it's custom, a server with sufficient resources to handle traffic spikes without going down. In 2026 these are the real options:

| Option | Approximate monthly price (COP) | Features | Ideal for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hostinger WooCommerce** | $35,000–$85,000 | SSD NVMe, LiteSpeed, free SSL, daily backups | Small store (< 5,000 visits/month) |
| **SiteGround GoGeek** | $120,000–$200,000 | Servers in USA, own optimizations for Woo, staging | Medium store (10K-50K visits/month) |
| **Cloudways VPS (DigitalOcean)** | $80,000–$250,000 | Managed VPS, scalable, integrated CDN | Growing store, variable traffic |
| **AWS Lightsail or EC2** | $120,000–$400,000 | Scalable infrastructure, pay-per-use, low latency | High traffic (> 100K visits/month) |
| **Colombian dedicated server** | $300,000–$800,000 | Physical server in Colombian datacenter (InterNexa, ColombiaHost) | Companies that require data in Colombia by regulation, low latency |

In all cases, for a WordPress e-commerce you need **SSL** (free with Let's Encrypt on most serious hostings), **daily automatic backups** (essential), and a **CDN** like Cloudflare (the free plan is more than enough for most stores).

A common mistake is contracting the cheapest hosting ($15,000/month) and then suffering crashes during high season. A slow or crashing e-commerce loses approximately **7% of sales** for each additional second of loading, according to Google studies. Don't skimp on hosting.

## 7. Hidden costs nobody tells you about

These are costs that appear after launching and most quotes don't include:

### 1. Professional product photography

If you sell physical products, catalog photos taken with a phone don't convert. A professional photography session in Bogotá for 50 products with white background costs between **$800,000 and $2,500,000 COP**. If products are large or require a model, the cost goes up.

### 2. Product description copywriting

Each SEO-optimized product sheet with persuasive descriptions can cost between **$15,000 and $40,000 COP per product** if you hire a specialized e-commerce copywriter. For 100 products, that's $1.5 to $4 million additional.

### 3. Integration with carriers

If your store needs to calculate shipping in real time with Servientrega, Coordinadora, or Envía, the API integration can cost between **$1.5 and $4 million additional**, depending on the number of carriers.

### 4. Legal compliance and data protection

In Colombia, every online store that collects personal data must comply with Law 1581 on Data Protection. You need:
- Privacy notice drafted by lawyer: **$300,000–$600,000 COP**
- Terms and conditions: **$400,000–$800,000 COP**
- Changes and returns policy: **$200,000–$400,000 COP**
- Database registration before the SIC (if applicable)

### 5. Advertising to generate traffic

An online store without traffic is a digital brochure nobody sees. You need budget for:
- Google Ads (Shopping, Search): minimum **$1,000,000/month** to see results
- Meta Ads (Instagram and Facebook): minimum **$800,000/month**
- SEO and content marketing: if you delegate it, **$1,500,000–$4,000,000/month**

Many entrepreneurs get frustrated because after investing $8 million in the store, no sales come in. The reason: they didn't budget for traffic acquisition.

### 6. Additional security certificate (PCI DSS)

If you handle credit cards and transaction volume is high, eventually you'll need PCI DSS certification. Gateways like Wompi and Stripe handle most of the compliance, but if your application touches card data (even without storing it), you need quarterly vulnerability scanning: **$300,000–$800,000 COP per scan**.

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What's the cheapest e-commerce I can make in Colombia in 2026?

You can launch a store on Shopify with the basic plan (USD $39/month) using a free theme and setting up products yourself. The .com.co domain costs about $45,000 COP per year. With that and Wompi integrated, your initial cost can be as low as **$500,000–$800,000 COP** (domain + first month + setup time). You don't need to pay anyone if you have the patience to learn. That said, the result won't be professional and probably won't convert as well as an optimized design, but to validate an idea it works.

For a store with custom design and professional optimization, prices start at **$6,000,000 COP** as I explained in the store types section.

### Shopify or WooCommerce for selling in Colombia?

For 2026, if your market is 100% Colombian and you plan to grow, **WooCommerce is usually better in the medium term** because you don't pay platform commissions and everything is billed in COP. If you also sell abroad, need to launch quickly, or don't want to deal with technical maintenance, **Shopify is an excellent option** despite the dollar cost.

The decision depends on three variables: your monthly recurring budget (Shopify is more expensive month to month), your need for customization (WooCommerce wins), and your tolerance for technical management (Shopify requires less). For a detailed comparison, review the [full table above](#comparison-table).

### How long does it take for an online store to be ready in Colombia?

It depends on scope. A Shopify store with less than 10 products and standard template can be ready in **3 to 5 days**. A WordPress e-commerce with custom design, 100+ products, and multiple gateways takes **4 to 8 weeks**. Custom development with complex integrations, **10 to 14 weeks**. Add one or two weeks of testing, adjustments, and training before official launch.

### Which payment gateway suits me best in Colombia?

If you can only choose one, **Wompi** is the best option for 100% national sales in 2026 due to its approval rate on Colombian cards, zero fixed monthly cost, and payout in 1 business day. If you sell abroad or need subscriptions, **Stripe** is superior in checkout experience and flexibility. The ideal is to integrate **at least two gateways** (for example, Wompi + Stripe) to maximize your checkout conversion rate. Each additional payment method you offer can increase sales between 5% and 15%.

### Is financing available to set up my e-commerce in Colombia?

Yes. Bancoldex has credit lines for SME digitalization with preferential rates. Bancolombia, Davivienda, and BBVA offer free investment credits that many entrepreneurs use to set up their e-commerce. There are also MinTIC and iNNpulsa calls with non-reimbursable resources for digital transformation (although competition is high). And many developers, including me, offer milestone payments: a percentage at start, another at progress, and the balance at launch, which makes the investment more manageable.

### Can I sell in Colombia without electronic invoicing?

It depends on your tax regime and product type. If you're a natural person with low income, you may not be obligated. But if you're a legal entity or your income exceeds a certain threshold, DIAN requires electronic invoicing. For 2026, the trend is that more and more businesses become obligated. I recommend consulting with a Colombian accountant before launching your store to avoid sanctions. If you need to integrate electronic invoicing in your e-commerce, providers like Alegra, Siigo, or FactureApp have APIs that can be connected to your store with an integration cost between $1,500,000 and $3,000,000 COP.
